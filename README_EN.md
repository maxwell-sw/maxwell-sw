## 💼 Project Experience

### 1. 16X Special Project of the Military Science Commission — World's Best-Performing Snowfield Camouflage Uniform with Multi-Band Stealth in UV, Visible, and IR
`First Author` `2025 – Present`

**Background**: Conventional snow camouflage only provides concealment in the visible band, but remains detectable under UV and infrared imaging. The key challenge is balancing high UV reflectance with low IR emissivity.

**Contribution**: I proposed a coupled design strategy combining a self-designed nanoporous infrared-transparent material with a low-emissivity layer to achieve coordinated modulation of electromagnetic waves across multiple bands. Achieved 90.8% UV reflectance, 80.2% visible reflectance, and 12.3% IR emissivity, enabling high-performance multi-band camouflage in snowfield environments.

`Jul 2026 – Sep 2026`

**Background**: Huawei's magnetoelectric storage solution offers high capacity, low cost, and low power consumption for data-center storage. **Contributions**:
