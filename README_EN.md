## 🧭 Overview

I study how materials data can become interpretable decisions. I am an M.S. candidate at Zhejiang University, with research experience in photonic thermal management, intelligent fibers, and multi-band optical regulation. I also turn material-research workflows into AI Agents with controlled tools for analysis and reporting.

> **Research thesis**: connect materials, data, and intelligent workflows to make complex analysis auditable and deliverable.

## ✦ Selected Work

### [Spectra Copilot](https://spectra-copilot.onrender.com/) · Auditable Material-Screening Agent

- An LLM + Harness Agent for material-spectrum screening. It turns natural-language requests into file parsing, condition confirmation, metric calculation, candidate ranking, and report delivery.
- Supports spectrum uploads, physical-quantity and unit confirmation, blackbody weighting, comparison plots, and HTML reports. Outputs distinguish tool facts, engineering judgments, and conditions requiring validation.
- [Live demo](https://spectra-copilot.onrender.com/) · [Source](https://github.com/1178922523-maker/spectra-copilot)

### Multi-band Snowfield Camouflage Materials

- First author. Developed a coupled design strategy using nanoporous infrared-transparent materials and low-emissivity layers for UV-visible-infrared camouflage.
- Achieved 90.8% UV reflectance, 80.2% visible reflectance, and 12.3% infrared emissivity for multi-band optical regulation in snowfield environments.

### Polar-Bear-Inspired Thermal-Management Aerogel Fibers and Textiles

- Participated in the structural design of bio-inspired ultra-thermal-insulation aerogel fibers. Related work was published in *Science & Technology Review*.

### Intelligent Sensing Fiber Thermal-Drawing System

- First author. Designed and manufactured a lab-scale thermal-drawing system for intelligent sensing fibers and fabricated millimeter-scale flexible sensing fibers.
